About PatchCast

PatchCast forecasts how strong each League of Legends champion will be on a patch, and keeps a public record of how right or wrong those forecasts were.

How it works

  • Strength. Each champion's recent and long-run winrate, each trusted in proportion to how many games it's based on, plus popularity.
  • Balance changes. Changes to base stats, ability numbers, cooldowns, item stats and item passives are turned into numbers (for example, "ability damage +8%") and weighted by what worked on past patches.
  • Items. Item changes are applied to champions in proportion to how often they build the item.
  • Testing. The model is always tested on patches it has never seen, the same way it's used.

Being honest about limits

The overall forecast is clearly better than just using last patch's winrate. Predicting the exact effect of a specific balance change is much harder: with only a couple of dozen patches of data, the patch-effect part is experimental and is switched off automatically whenever testing says it isn't helping. More games per patch is the main thing that would improve it.

Data

Ranked match data collected through the Riot Games API, champion and item data from Data Dragon and CommunityDragon. PatchCast shows champion-level statistics only: no individual players are shown or rated.
